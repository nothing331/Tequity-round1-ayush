2. When a portal user creates a new record, how can we be sure it doesn't get created twice if the network drops halfway? Does Salesforce let us attach our own ID to a record (an external ID) and "create or update" by it, or is there another way you'd suggest?
3. When we create a record in Salesforce, what does it send back to us: the new record ID, a success or failure, a timestamp? And when the webhook for that same change arrives, how will we be able to tell it was our own change and not something a sales person did?
4. How can we be sure a webhook really came from Salesforce? Is there a shared secret or signature we can check, since the endpoint is open to the public internet?
5. How reliable is the ordering and timing of webhooks? Is there a version or sequence number per record, how precise is the occurredAt time, and how far apart can Salesforce's clock and ours be?
6. If our endpoint is down or Salesforce is unreachable, does Salesforce retry its webhooks, and for how long? After the 6-hour outage, can we ask Salesforce which records changed during that time, or are those changes simply lost?
7. How do the first 5 million records get into the portal at the start? Is there a separate bulk load, or would that all go through the normal record API?
8. Can a contact ever reach us before the customer it belongs to? If it can, how would you want us to handle it?
9. How long do we need to keep the history of changes, and who will look at it? Is it checked record by record, or searched across everything?
10. What traffic can we expect at peak and low time for these request and how many simultaneous request can we expect?
